Uriel Oil and Gas · Energy

Excellence in energy.

A full-service oil trading company, incorporated in Nigeria in 2008. We trade, market and distribute crude oil and petroleum products, backed by marine logistics and terminal operations.

Talk to our energy team
The company

Who we are

We have exported crude oil and refined products from Nigerian refineries and from selected locations along the West African coast. Over the years our management has built relationships with some of the largest players in international oil, alongside a strong network of local contacts.

Marketing and distribution of oil products are a significant part of our business. We have registered offices in Lagos and Abuja, with our headquarters in Lagos.

About Uriel

Building since 2008.

Uriel began in 2008 as an oil and gas trading company and built its first homes in Lekki a year later. Today the group has five companies and has delivered more than 160 homes in Ikoyi, Victoria Island and Lekki, along with roads, schools and hospital works for Lagos State, Oyo State and the Federal Government.
